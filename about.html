The Association

Industrial Mathematics Students' Association

The Industrial Mathematics Students' Association (IMSA) is a society dedicated to enhancing the knowledge and practical applications of mathematics among its members. Collaborating with industry, IMSA promotes industrial mathematics and its relevance in real-world problem-solving.

The association gives mathematics undergraduates of the Faculty a platform to connect, exchange ideas and learn from one another, bridging the gap between academia and industry and preparing members for successful careers in industrial mathematics and related fields. IMSA also shares mathematics knowledge with the general public through activities that support mathematics learners, including school children.
